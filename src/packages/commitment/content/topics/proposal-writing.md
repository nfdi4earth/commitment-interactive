## The data management section {statement=7}

Most funders ask how you will handle data. Statement 7 asks you to adopt a DMP – write the proposal section as a short DMP, e.g. with [RDMO](https://rdmorganiser.github.io/) or your *funder's template*.

## Name your target repository {statement=5}

Reviewers like concrete plans. Name the ESS domain repository where you will publish data (statement 5), and why it fits your data types.

## Budget for curation {statement=10}

Data publication and curation take time and sometimes money. Statement 10 asks us to sustain repositories – include these costs in your budget.

## Legal aspects {statement=3}

Plan for data laws early (statement 3): personal data, INSPIRE-relevant data or third-party data need a strategy before the project starts.

## Use NFDI services {statement=1}

Mentioning existing NFDI4Earth services (statement 1) shows you build on community infrastructure instead of reinventing it.
