[Example content] **Data experts** are the people who make FAIR data possible: data curators, data stewards, repository managers, research software engineers and data scientists in infrastructure teams.

Their work is often invisible in publications and evaluations. Valuing them means:

1. **crediting** them, e.g. with the [CRediT](https://credit.niso.org/) role *Data curation*
2. **involving** them early in projects
3. **creating career paths** for them at institutions

Statements 10–12 spell out how: sustaining repositories, collaborating with curators and building on shared standards.
