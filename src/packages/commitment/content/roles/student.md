You are starting to work with data – in coursework, a thesis or as a student assistant.

## Statement 3

Check whether your thesis data includes **personal** or *restricted* data.

- [ ] Ask your supervisor about data agreements

## Statement 4

Cite datasets and software in your thesis like papers.

- [ ] Find the DOI of one dataset you use

## Statement 5

Publish your thesis data in a domain repository, not only as a PDF appendix.

- [ ] Look up the repository your group uses

## Statement 6

Join a data literacy course or summer school.

- [ ] Bookmark one OER course

## Statement 7

Write a one-page DMP for your thesis.

- [ ] Use the DMP template of your university

## Statement 8

Pick CC BY 4.0 for your data and MIT for your scripts.

- [ ] Read the licensing deep dive
