“As open as possible, as closed as necessary” – how openness runs through the whole commitment.

## A thread, not a statement

No single statement is “the openness statement”. Openness appears in the preamble, in statements 1, 2, 6 and 8, and implicitly in the call for sharing.

## Open is a spectrum

Between closed and fully open there are many steps: embargoed, restricted access, open with attribution, public domain. The commitment asks you to move as far to the open end as you responsibly can.

## Openness and FAIR

FAIR data is not necessarily open, and open data is not necessarily FAIR. The commitment asks for both.

## Openness for institutions

Institutions enable openness through policies, infrastructure and recognition in research evaluation (DORA, CoARA).
