You collect, analyse and publish data, write proposals and review the work of others.

## Statement 1

Mention relevant NFDI4Earth services in your proposals.

- [ ] Search OneStop4All for a service you could use

## Statement 2

Know which declarations your institution has signed.

- [ ] Check if your institution signed DORA

## Statement 3

Plan legal checks in your DMP.

- [ ] List data with access restrictions

## Statement 4

Cite data and software in the reference list.

- [ ] Add data citations to your next paper

## Statement 5

Choose a domain repository that covers all four FAIR aspects.

- [ ] Compare two repositories via the Label

## Statement 7

Write and update a DMP for every project.

- [ ] Schedule a DMP review at month 12

## Statement 8

Default to CC BY 4.0 / CC0 for data, permissive licences for code.

- [ ] Add a LICENSE file to your repos

## Statement 10

Budget data publication costs in proposals.

- [ ] Add a curation line to your next budget

## Statement 11

Involve curators early and credit them.

- [ ] Contact a repository before data collection

## Statement 12

Use community formats and metadata standards.

- [ ] Check your data against CF conventions
