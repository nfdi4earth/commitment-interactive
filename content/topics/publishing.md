## Data first {statement=5}

Deposit the data in a domain repository before you submit the paper, so the data DOI can go into the data availability statement.

## Choose a licence {statement=8}

Pick an open licence for data (CC BY 4.0 / CC0) and code (e.g. MIT). See the licensing deep dive.

## Cite your sources {statement=4}

Cite all datasets and software you used in the reference list – including your own.

## Work with curators {statement=11}

Repository curators will check your submission. Plan time for their feedback.

## Use standard formats {statement=12}

Community formats make your data reusable – and reviewers increasingly check this.

## Check legal restrictions {statement=3}

Make sure you are allowed to publish all data, especially third-party or personal data.
