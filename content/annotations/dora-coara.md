The **San Francisco Declaration on Research Assessment** (DORA) and the **Coalition for Advancing Research Assessment** (CoARA) call for evaluating research on its merits rather than on journal-based metrics.

For you this may mean: list datasets and software in your CV, and use narrative CVs where offered.
