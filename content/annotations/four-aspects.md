Many repositories are good at **F** and **A** (DOIs, landing pages) but weaker at **I** and **R** (machine-readable formats, vocabularies, licences, provenance).

The commitment asks you to choose repositories that cover all four. The NFDI4Earth Label is one way to see this at a glance.
