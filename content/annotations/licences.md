A licence tells others what they may do with your data or code. Without one, the default is *all rights reserved* – even if the file is publicly downloadable.

Common open choices:

- **Data:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) or [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- **Software:** permissive licences such as [MIT](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/mit/) or [Apache-2.0](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/apache-2.0/)

Avoid “non-commercial” (NC) and “no derivatives” (ND) clauses for research data – they block reuse in many projects.
